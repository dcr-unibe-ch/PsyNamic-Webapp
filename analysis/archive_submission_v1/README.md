# Archive: submitted analysis (submission v1)

Snapshot of the manuscript analysis as submitted, taken on 8 Nov 2026 before recomputing it on the re-predicted data (cutoff 31 Oct 2026, new models).

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `export/` | Database exports (`data/export.py`) of the submitted database. The `_2025` files are the 5,651 studies of the manuscript (`entrez_year <= 2025` or `NULL`). |
| `plots/` | Figures generated by `analysis_pubmed_new.ipynb` from those exports. |
| `duplicates_title.csv` | Title duplicates found during the manual merge (cell 31). |
| `analysis_pubmed_new.ipynb`, `analysis_substance_new.ipynb` | Notebooks as submitted, with their outputs (= numbers quoted in the manuscript). |
| `analysis_pubmed_new_post_revision.ipynb` | Work-in-progress copy (paths updated to `data/analysis/` and `data/history/`), with its outputs as of 6 Nov 2026. |

The old seed and prediction files are in `data/history/`.
